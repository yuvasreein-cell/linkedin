Frequently Asked Questions

How does the ₹1 audit work? +
The ₹1 audit is a comprehensive review of your LinkedIn profile. We analyze your profile completeness, positioning clarity, content strategy, and personal brand presence. You'll receive detailed feedback on what's working, what needs improvement, and specific action steps to take.
Is this for me if I already have a large following? +
Absolutely! Many high-follower accounts underperform because they lack strategic direction. We focus on engagement quality, content consistency, and brand authority—not just follower count. Large followings often benefit the most from our strategy work.
Do you write the posts for me? +
Yes! We provide done-for-you content scripts that maintain your authentic voice while optimizing for LinkedIn's algorithm. Every post is designed to resonate with your audience and drive meaningful engagement.
How long does it take to see results? +
Most clients see noticeable improvements in profile views and engagement within 2-4 weeks of implementing the strategy. Significant growth in followers and opportunities typically follows within 3-6 months, depending on consistency.
What industries do you work with? +
We've worked with consultants, founders, executives, coaches, and professionals across tech, finance, consulting, healthcare, and more. Our strategy is industry-agnostic and tailored to your specific niche and goals.
Can you guarantee leads? +
While we can't guarantee specific lead numbers, our clients consistently report increased inbound inquiries due to improved credibility, visibility, and strategic positioning. Results depend on implementation and market fit.
How much time will this take me per week? +
We handle the heavy lifting with content creation and strategy. You typically need 2-3 hours per week for engagement and community building. We provide systems to make this efficient and sustainable.
What's the difference between this and just posting regularly? +
Random posting rarely builds authority. Our approach combines strategic positioning, content themes aligned to your expertise, optimized posting schedules, and engagement tactics. It's the difference between noise and signal on LinkedIn.
